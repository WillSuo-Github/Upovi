The authoritative terms and required copyright notice are in [LICENSE](../LICENSE); the [official license text](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0) explains the permitted purposes.

The license allows noncommercial purposes and the personal and organizational uses it expressly lists, together with modification and redistribution under its conditions. Commercial purposes outside those permissions require a separate license from the relevant copyright holders. Distribution must carry the license or its URL and the required notices. This summary does not replace the full terms.

A license with a noncommercial restriction is **source-available**, rather than open source under the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd). Project descriptions should use that terminology.

## Third-party components

Third-party code, libraries, headers and notices retain their own licenses, as documented in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) and the individual files. In particular, Sparkle and mas retain their MIT terms, and the libarchive headers retain their BSD-style terms. The Upovi license change does not remove those upstream permissions or notices.

## Contributions and distribution

New contributions are accepted under the terms described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Contributors retain their copyright. Commercial permission must come from the copyright holders with authority over the material in question.

Future application builds include the current project license as `Contents/Resources/Upovi-LICENSE` and the third-party notice index as `Contents/Resources/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, alongside the existing upstream licenses. Their About text and bundle copyright metadata identify PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. This repository change does not itself publish or replace an application release.
